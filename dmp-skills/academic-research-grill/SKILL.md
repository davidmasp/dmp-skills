# Academic Research Grill

Run a `/grilling` session.

Keep the academic project’s intended outcome as the anchor. In addition to the normal grilling process:

- Clarify what “done” means and what the work must produce or demonstrate.
- Turn the goal into a focused research question, hypothesis, or claim.
- Identify the necessary evidence, method, data, and constraints.
- Sequence the required steps and expose dependencies; separate must-haves from nice-to-haves.
- Challenge unsupported assumptions, feasibility risks, alternative explanations, and weak links between claims and evidence.
- End with a concise, confirmed plan and clear stopping criteria.
